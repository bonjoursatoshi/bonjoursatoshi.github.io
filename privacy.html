Bonjour Satoshi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026 · version française du site

Bonjour Satoshi is a French daily crypto news show. The publishing application ("the App") is an internal desktop tool operated solely by the owner of the Bonjour Satoshi channels.

1. Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal data from any user. It has no public users: its only user is the operator of Bonjour Satoshi.

2. Third-party platforms

The App uses the TikTok Content Posting API exclusively to upload our own original videos to our own TikTok account. The only data exchanged are OAuth tokens for our own account and the video files we produce. No viewer or follower data is accessed.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
